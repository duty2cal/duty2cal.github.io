Duty plan to calendar

Turn one Lufthansa cabin-crew duty plan published as a Jeppesen PDF export into a calendar file. The plan stays in this tab.

Before you choose a plan

  • This tool converts a Lufthansa cabin-crew duty plan published as a Jeppesen PDF export into one calendar file. One roster month of cabin crew; not cockpit duties, other Lufthansa duty plans, other Jeppesen exports or another operator.
  • Convert here, in a current browser, with no installation.
  • Downloads for various operating systems are available on request; the release page carries the published builds.
  • Processing happens in your browser: no file is uploaded, nothing is stored, and no request is sent after the page loads.
  • Not affiliated with Lufthansa, Jeppesen, or any duty-plan provider or airline.
  • The software is provided as is, without warranty of any kind.
